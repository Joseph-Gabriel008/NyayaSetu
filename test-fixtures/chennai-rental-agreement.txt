RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Chennai on this 1st day of October 2024, by and between:

Mr. R. Sundaram, residing at Old No. 14, New No. 28, 4th Main Road, Gandhi Nagar, Adyar, Chennai - 600020, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR / LANDLORD" (which expression shall unless excluded by or repugnant to the context mean and include his legal heirs, executors, and administrators) of the ONE PART;

AND

Ms. Priya Natarajan, D/o K. Natarajan, permanently residing at No. 5, Bharathi Street, Madurai - 625001, and currently employed in Chennai, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE / TENANT" (which expression shall unless excluded by or repugnant to the context mean and include her legal representatives) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing Door No. 2B, Second Floor, Marvel Apartments, Velachery Main Road, Chennai - 600042 (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULE PREMISES").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TENURE AND PURPOSE
The tenancy shall be for an 11-month period commencing from 1st October 2024 to 31st August 2025. The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant only and no other occupants or paying guests are permitted without written sanction.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 26,000/- (Rupees Twenty Six Thousand Only) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via NEFT/UPI. In case of delay, a late penalty charge of Rs. 500/- per day shall be levied without exception. In addition to rent, monthly apartment society maintenance of Rs. 3,500/- shall be borne solely by the Tenant.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DEDUCTIONS
The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 2,60,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs Sixty Thousand Only, equivalent to 10 months' rent). The Lessor reserves the right to retain the deposit for up to 90 days after vacating. The Lessor shall unilaterally deduct a mandatory 1-month rent towards painting and repainting irrespective of actual wall conditions, alongside any repair expenses assessed solely by the Lessor.

4. ANNUAL RENT ESCALATION
In the event of renewal of this agreement after 11 months, the monthly rent shall automatically escalate by 10% (ten percent) without negotiation.

5. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY
The Landlord or his authorized representatives shall have unfettered right to enter, inspect, and display the Schedule Premises to prospective buyers or tenants at any time without prior written or oral notice. The Tenant shall surrender one master duplicate key to the Landlord.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall maintain the interior premises in good tenantable order. The Tenant agrees to bear the costs of all repairs, whether minor or structural (including plumbing, seepage, motor pump repairs, and electrical fixtures), occurring during the tenancy, regardless of original age or pre-existing defects.

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
The Landlord may terminate this tenancy at any time without assigning any reason by giving 7 (seven) days' written notice to the Tenant, upon which the Tenant must vacate immediately. If the Tenant wishes to terminate the tenancy prior to the completion of the 11-month tenure, the Tenant must forfeit the entire security deposit of Rs. 2,60,000/- as liquidated damages.

8. PROHIBITIONS AND CONDUCT
The Tenant shall not keep any pets, shall not cook non-vegetarian food, and shall not permit guests of the opposite gender or overnight visitors. Violation of any of these house rules shall result in immediate eviction within 24 hours without refund of deposit.

9. JURISDICTION AND DISPUTES
In the event of any disputes or claims arising under this agreement, the courts in Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction. The Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act (TNRRRL Act) provisions shall be superseded by the terms of this private covenant.
