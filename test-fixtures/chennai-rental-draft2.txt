RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (REVISED NEGOTIATED DRAFT)

This Rental Agreement is made and executed at Chennai on this 5th day of October 2024, by and between:

Mr. R. Sundaram, residing at Old No. 14, New No. 28, 4th Main Road, Gandhi Nagar, Adyar, Chennai - 600020, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR / LANDLORD" of the ONE PART;

AND

Ms. Priya Natarajan, D/o K. Natarajan, permanently residing at No. 5, Bharathi Street, Madurai - 625001, and currently employed in Chennai, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE / TENANT" of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing Door No. 2B, Second Floor, Marvel Apartments, Velachery Main Road, Chennai - 600042 (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULE PREMISES").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TENURE AND PURPOSE
The tenancy shall be for an 11-month period commencing from 1st October 2024 to 31st August 2025. The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and immediate family or declared roommates.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 26,000/- (Rupees Twenty Six Thousand Only) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via NEFT/UPI. In case of delay beyond a 5-day grace period, a late fee of Rs. 100/- per day shall apply. Monthly apartment society maintenance of Rs. 3,500/- shall be paid by the Tenant against valid society receipts.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DEDUCTIONS
The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 78,000/- (Rupees Seventy Eight Thousand Only, equivalent to 3 months' rent, in accordance with the Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act). The Lessor shall refund the entire deposit via bank transfer on the day of handing over keys. Actual painting deductions shall only occur if the walls are damaged beyond ordinary wear and tear, supported by verified vendor invoices.

4. ANNUAL RENT ESCALATION
In the event of renewal of this agreement after 11 months upon mutual agreement, the monthly rent escalation shall not exceed 5% (five percent).

5. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY
The Landlord or his authorized representatives may inspect the premises during reasonable daytime hours (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM) only after providing at least 24 hours prior written notice to the Tenant. The Tenant shall have exclusive possession and privacy.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall bear routine minor wear-and-tear repairs up to Rs. 1,000/- per instance. All major and structural repairs (including seepage, structural plumbing, electrical wiring defects, and motor pump replacement) shall be the sole responsibility and expense of the Landlord.

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this tenancy by serving 1 (one) month written notice or paying 1 month's rent in lieu of notice. Upon expiry of notice and settlement of outstanding dues, the security deposit shall be refunded in full without forfeiture.

8. CODE OF CONDUCT AND GUESTS
The Tenant shall maintain peaceful and quiet enjoyment of the premises. Reasonable social guests are permitted with reasonable discretion. No arbitrary or discriminatory dietary or lifestyle restrictions shall be imposed.

9. JURISDICTION AND STATUTORY COMPLIANCE
This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India and the Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act (TNRRRL Act), 2017. Both parties agree to register this agreement on the official TN Tenancy portal.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have set their respective hands on the date first above mentioned.
